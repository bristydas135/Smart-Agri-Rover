# Robotic Arm

## Overview

The rover uses a 6-degree-of-freedom robotic arm for vegetable harvesting.

## Specifications

- 6 degrees of freedom
- Aluminum construction
- Six MG996R servo motors
- Two-finger V-shaped claw
- Blade-assisted cutting mechanism

## Harvesting Process

1. Detect the tomato using the camera.
2. Determine tomato ripeness.
3. Position the rover.
4. Position the robotic arm.
5. Grasp the tomato.
6. Cut the branch.
7. Collect the tomato.

## Inverse Kinematics

An analytical inverse-kinematics approach is used to determine the required joint angles for a desired end-effector position.
